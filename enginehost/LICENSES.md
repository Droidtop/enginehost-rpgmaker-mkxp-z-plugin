# RPG Maker RGSS engine bundle notices

This bundle is built from mkxp-z plus the Android integration derived from
thehatkid's mkxp-z-android port. The engine is distributed under GPL-2.0-or-
later; its dependencies and the two bundled Ruby runtimes retain their own
upstream notices and licenses. `COPYING`, `THIRD_PARTY.md`, the dependency
sources, and their included license files are authoritative.

Ruby 3.1.3 and the official Ruby 1.9.2-p320 source are compiled as separately
namespaced runtimes. Enginehost selects one capability for a session; it does
not replace, translate, or redistribute any game scripts or assets.
